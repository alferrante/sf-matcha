The additional Pistachio Cream +$1 modifier is an optional extra, not necessary to purchase the listed pistachio-cream drink.

Binge’s current platform includes an AI-description warning. Only the documented item name, current displayed price and location-specific ordering schedule are used. The attractive prose about ceremonial matcha, real pistachio foam and milk-of-choice is deliberately not treated as proof of recipe, origin or allergens. Binge’s own website primarily describes its Berkeley locations and links to Berkeley Snackpass ordering, so it must not be cited as proof of the SF schedule or pistachio item. The guide does not claim oat, almond or soy availability for this exact order. Neither drink is presented as dairy-free or allergen-safe. Pistachio is a tree nut; customers needing allergy assurances should ask the café directly.
