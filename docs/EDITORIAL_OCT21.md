# Editorial release — October 21, 2026

## Published guide

**Where to get matcha einspänner in SF** is a compact, unranked guide to four operating San Francisco cafés with a specifically named matcha einspänner on a current official or merchant menu.

| Café | Menu evidence | Address | Dairy / soy note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoji | Ceremonial matcha with lightly sweetened whipped cream; current official price not published | 140 New Montgomery St, Suite 1 | Whipped cream is explicit; soy and a dairy-free version are not published |
| Ceré Tea | Matcha Einspänner, $8.99 on Uber Eats; flavored versions $9.49–$9.99 | 1186 Valencia St | Cream or whipped toppings are listed; soy is available for matcha, but the topping is not confirmed dairy-free |
| Kiyomi Cà Phê Pop-Up | Matcha Einspanner, $9.75 on DoorDash | 1439 Taraval St, inside Lost & Found | Condensed milk and salted cream foam; soy is not listed |
| The Buoy | Matcha Einspanner, $7.50 on Uber Eats | 22 Franklin St | Uber Eats lists dairy cream; soy is not listed, and ingredients should be confirmed for allergen needs |

Sources were checked October 21, 2026. Delivery prices can differ from counter prices. Kiyomi remains a pop-up, so the guide tells readers to check its Instagram before visiting. The guide makes no tasting, ranking, dairy-free or partnership claim.
