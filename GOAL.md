# SF Matcha: 90-day growth goal

Start: October 7, 2026. Target date: January 5, 2027.

Make sanfranciscomatcha.com the default destination for discovering matcha in San Francisco. This file is the execution contract for sprint runs, not proof that native Goal mode is enabled.

## Outcome targets

| Metric | January 5 target | Evidence required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Monthly site visits | 50,000 | Analytics export with defined visit metric and trailing 30-day window |
| Email subscribers | 10,000 | Provider export; active subscribers, no duplicates |
| Combined social followers | 25,000 | Dated counts for identified SF Matcha accounts |
| Published editorial guides | 24 | Live canonical URLs, sources, verification dates |
| Derivative social assets | 120 | Finished assets and distribution status, counted separately |
| Live café offers | 20 | Written merchant confirmation, live offer, terms and expiry |
| Offer redemptions | 2,000 | Successful redemption records, reconciled with merchants |
| Verified awards votes | 1,000 | Validated, deduplicated votes |

Audience and conversion numbers are stretch targets, not promised results. Establish baselines and tracking before reporting gains. Unknown is not zero.

## Delivery checkpoints

- October 21: current source reconciled; source build and cache fix shipped; dynamic proof points and working email capture live.
- November 6: guides hub and six evidence-backed guides live; transparent badge methodology; partner-ready $1-off offer flow.
- November 21: five merchant-confirmed offers live; four pilot award categories ready with real tasting evidence or explicitly awaiting that evidence.
- January 5: 24 guides and 120 derivative assets delivered, with progress audited against all growth targets.

## Working cadence

- Weekdays: growth lead completes the highest-impact 1–3 unblocked actions and updates `SPRINT_SCOREBOARD.md`.
- Mondays: existing venue watcher verifies and publishes factual directory changes.
- Tuesdays and Fridays: editorial packages, beginning with banana matcha, cold foam, and dairy-free matcha.
- Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays: ongoing café partner research and pipeline advancement.
- First Friday monthly: badge/award evidence review.

Use sub-agents for independent research or implementation when useful. Keep one publisher and avoid concurrent edits to the same files. Pull current main before each change, preserve unrelated work, run relevant tests and the customer-copy audit, rebuild all changed source, then publish and verify the result. Do not stop at planning when implementation is unblocked.

## Editorial and partner constraints

Preserve the exclusions and planned-location rules in README.md. Never invent firsthand tasting, texture, sweetness, popularity or winner claims. A research-only guide may list verified options and attributed descriptions; tasting-based rankings and Best awards need real tasting evidence. Do not tie awards to payments or offers. Do not contact cafés without explicit authorization. Publish discounts only after merchant confirmation. Never invent metrics, active email capture, redemption tracking or provider integration.
