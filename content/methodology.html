How we choose
matcha spots.

Listings

We check addresses, status, menus, hours and milk options against linked sources. Planned spots need a verified address and matcha menu. Details can change.

Labels

✦ Buzzy

An opening, distinctive matcha program or recent coverage. Each label needs a specific reason, source, verification date and review within 90 days. Expired labels are hidden.

Buzzy isn’t a taste or popularity rating.

⭐ Top Pick

Our original shortlist

The original directory’s shortlist, not awards or head-to-head tasting winners. New picks will explain their basis.

Milk options

Soy confirmed

Soy reported
Reported by a secondary source; not independently confirmed.
No soy listed / Soy TBD
Unverified or incomplete. Ask the café.

Check toppings separately: plant milk doesn’t make foams or condensed milk dairy-free.

“Ceremonial” reflects the café’s description, not our quality score.

Awards

No winners yet. Planned categories: banana matcha, cold foam, dairy-free options and value.

“Best” awards need dated firsthand tastings, named drinks and clear criteria. Notes record milk, sweetness, toppings and price; judging covers flavor, balance, preparation and category-specific qualities.

Menu-researched guides aren’t taste rankings. Reader-voted awards will be separate, with dates and voting checks.

Independence

Offers, sponsors and samples can’t buy labels or awards. Articles disclose relevant paid relationships and complimentary tastings.

We date reviews and correct claims when sources change.
