how the pilot will work

  1. Choose a café with an active, confirmed offer and read its terms.
  2. Get a voucher through the offer’s claim page. Availability is checked before a voucher is issued.
  3. Show the voucher to café staff before paying. Staff verify it and apply $1 off one eligible matcha.

One use per café per customer during the pilot. Each café sets its eligible drinks, expiry, exclusions and redemption cap. Offers may pause or end when the cap is reached. Vouchers become available after the café confirms the offer and its voucher system is ready.

You’ll see any newsletter invitation separately from voucher delivery.
