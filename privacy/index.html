Privacy

Email signup

Signup requires consent. Your email, consent, date and signup source go to our Render-hosted service and Resend. Signup saves your subscription without sending a confirmation email. We don’t sell addresses or share them with cafés for marketing.

Newsletter emails will include an unsubscribe link. We may retain an opt-out record to prevent accidental resubscription. Don’t enter payment details or sensitive information.

Café offer drafts

The planner works in your browser. It doesn’t send café details to us, enroll a café or activate an offer. Any future voucher service will provide terms and privacy details before collecting customer information.

Site services

Google Maps (when enabled), Google Fonts and Cloudflare Web Analytics receive ordinary browser requests under their own privacy policies.

Cloudflare · Google · Resend

Privacy requests

Contact the maintainer. Issues are public: don’t post your email or personal information. Ask for a private follow-up. For data held by Resend, email support@resend.com.
