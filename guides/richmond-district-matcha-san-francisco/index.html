Matcha in SF’s Richmond District: Clement and Balboa Stops

Compare three Inner and Outer Richmond matcha cafés, from Clement Street lattes to Constance’s freshly milled matcha on Balboa.

Updated October 8, 2026

The Richmond’s matcha options run from Clement Street coffee shops to a tea studio farther west on Balboa. Kiss of Matcha lists a hand-whisked latte and a large flavored-matcha menu. Pixlcat pairs an Uji matcha program with its butter mochi. Constance mills tencha leaves into matcha in house. These three stops cover both Inner and Outer Richmond; choose by drink style and location, rather than treating them as a ranking.

We checked official websites and café ordering menus on October 8, 2026. Prices are published menu prices before tax, sizes and extras. Kiss of Matcha’s weekly schedule comes from its ordering page’s published location data. Constance has limited public menu detail, so its prices and milk choices remain unconfirmed here. Menu listings do not guarantee stock.

Matcha Latte

Kiss of Matcha - Clement

750 Clement St · Inner Richmond · San Francisco

For hand-whisked matcha with an optional cream layer.

The Clement shop is at 750 Clement St. Its latte description lists six grams of hand-whisked matcha over whole milk or a choice of milk. The wider menu also includes strawberry, mango, ube, azuki and jasmine matcha lattes.

Milk details
Whole milk is the named base. Other milk choices are offered but not named on the public product page, so ask specifically about soy. The optional matcha cream is explicitly dairy.
Soy status
Soy milk is available for lattes. The Matcha Cloud foam contains whole milk and cream, so choose a standard matcha latte for a soy-based drink.

Matcha Latte $7.47 on the ordering menu

Published location data lists Tue–Thu 10am–8pm, Fri–Sat 10am–10pm and Sun 10am–9pm, with no Monday hours. Check the current ordering page before visiting.

Matcha Latte or Iced Strawberry Matcha

Pixlcat Coffee

519 Clement St · Inner Richmond · San Francisco

For an unsweetened base latte with confirmed oat and almond options.

Pixlcat’s 519 Clement St menu identifies its matcha as organic ceremonial Uji matcha. The classic latte is unsweetened by default; the fruit menu includes strawberry and guava. Matcha Green Tea Mochi is listed separately at $5.25 on the ordering menu.

Milk details
The classic latte panel lists oat substitution and almond substitution (+$1). Soy is not listed. The Matcha Dopo Panna includes whipped cream even when a plant milk is chosen; ask about dairy in mochi and flavored additions separately.

Soy availability for matcha is not listed.

Constance Tea

3512 Balboa St · Outer Richmond · San Francisco

For an Outer Richmond tea studio that mills matcha in house.

Constance’s official website identifies matcha lattes and freshly milled matcha at 3512 Balboa St. It describes grinding tencha leaves in house with Japanese stone mills. Its public site does not provide a detailed drinks menu, so check the current selection at the counter.

Before you go

Which Richmond matcha latte has confirmed plant-milk options?

Pixlcat’s classic Matcha Latte ordering panel explicitly lists oat and almond substitutions. Kiss of Matcha mentions a choice of milk without naming those options on its public product page; Constance does not publish milk choices on its current website.

Does choosing oat milk make a cream-topped drink dairy-free?

Check the topping as well as the base. Kiss of Matcha labels its optional matcha cream as dairy, and Pixlcat’s Matcha Dopo Panna description includes whipped cream. A plant-milk substitution alone does not remove those ingredients.

Are all three Richmond stops in the same area?

Pixlcat and Kiss of Matcha are on Clement Street in the Inner Richmond. Constance is farther west on Balboa Street in the Outer Richmond. Use each listing’s map pin to plan travel between them.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
