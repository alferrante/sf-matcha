Ceremonial & Hand-Whisked Matcha in San Francisco

Three SF cafés documenting matcha sourcing or hand-whisking, with simple tea orders and latte options.

Updated October 7, 2026

If the tea itself is what you came for, start with preparation and sourcing details rather than a colorful topping. Stonemill names a hand-whisked order, Nagomi describes hand-whisking Shizuoka matcha, and Pixlcat identifies the Kyoto Uji matcha used for its lattes. These are three different ways to explore the tea. A sourcing description can help you choose an order; it does not replace tasting it yourself.

Official café pages checked October 7, 2026. “Ceremonial” is reported as the café describes its matcha; we do not treat that word alone as an independent quality award. Hand-whisking is identified only where the café explicitly documents it.

Before you go

Does ceremonial-grade automatically mean best?

No. We report the café’s description and link its source. We have not independently scored the matcha or awarded a winner.

Which café explicitly lists hand-whisked matcha?

Stonemill names a Hand Whisked Matcha order. Nagomi also describes hand-whisking its matcha on its official site.

How can I compare the tea without flavored cream?

Ask about a simple water-based matcha order or a plain latte. Use the same milk and sweetness preference when comparing cafés.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
