Matcha Under $7 in San Francisco

Four SF matcha menus with options below $7 before tax, including plant-milk prices and a matcha soda.

Updated October 7, 2026

A matcha order can stay below $7 if you watch size, temperature and milk surcharges. These four official menus list qualifying drinks without needing a promo code. The prices below are before tax, tip and extras. Paper Son also has a milk-free-style soda build, while Il Parco publishes enough size and milk detail to plan a soy order. Check the checkout total before paying; this guide uses the menu prices available on the update date.

Official menus checked October 7, 2026. Every featured base drink is listed strictly below $7. This is a price guide, not a Best Value taste award. Delivery prices and fees can differ from café prices.

Before you go

Do these prices include tax?

No. The guide compares published menu prices before tax, tip, optional extras and delivery fees.

Which plant-milk orders stay under $7?

The listed Il Parco 12-ounce latte with soy, oat or almond and Paper Son latte with oat or almond stay below $7 before tax. SŌHN lists free oat or almond alternatives.

Does a $1-off SF Matcha code already work here?

No offer is implied by inclusion. A café discount is valid only when an active offer explicitly lists its participating café and terms.
