Where to Get Banana Matcha in San Francisco

Three SF banana matcha drinks, from oat-milk lattes to seasonal injeolmi foam, with menu prices and milk notes.

Updated October 7, 2026

Banana matcha comes in a few different forms: banana in the milk, banana in the drink, or banana underneath a cloud of cream. These three San Francisco menus give you distinct versions to try. SŌHN has the oat-milk version, YakiniQ lists a straightforward iced banana matcha, and Kumo has a seasonal banana-and-kinako build. Pick by ingredients and neighborhood before deciding which one is your favorite.

We checked café menus on October 7, 2026. This is an unranked guide to documented drinks, not a tasting-based “best” list. Prices are listed menu prices before tax and extras; a menu listing does not guarantee stock.

Banana Oat Milk Latte with Matcha

SŌHN

2535 3rd St · Dogpatch · San Francisco

For banana oat milk without a cream-top order.

SŌHN lists matcha as one of the tea or coffee choices for its iced banana oat milk latte. Go to 2535 3rd St in Dogpatch.

Milk details
Oat milk is named in this drink; soy is not listed. Confirm any additional ingredients if you avoid dairy.
Soy status
Banana milk and oat milk are available; soy availability is not listed.

Kumo Matcha Pop-Up

For banana purée, soy cloud and kinako together.

Before you go

Which banana matcha uses oat milk?

SŌHN explicitly lists a Banana Oat Milk Latte with a matcha option. If dairy avoidance matters, confirm the full recipe when ordering.

Is Kumo’s soy-cloud banana matcha dairy-free?

No. Its official menu marks the Banana Injeolmi Matcha Latte as containing dairy and says its milk drinks use lactose-free milk.

Are these ranked from best to worst?

No. The list helps you compare documented menus. We have not awarded a tasting winner.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
