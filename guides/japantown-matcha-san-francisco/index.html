Where to Get Matcha in San Francisco Japantown

Three Japantown matcha stops for hand-whisked tea, fruit lattes and soft serve, with addresses, menu prices and milk caveats.

Updated October 8, 2026

Japantown gives you several ways to order matcha within a small area. Best Boy Electric lists unsweetened hand-whisked lattes and usucha on Post Street. YakiniQ adds banana, strawberry, red bean and yuzu to its matcha menu. Maiko’s Japan Center shop is a stop for a latte or matcha dessert. Use the menu styles below to plan your visit; these cafés are listed without a tasting ranking.

We checked official café menus, ordering pages and location pages on October 8, 2026. NEW PEOPLE, Best Boy Electric’s host, supplies its posted café hours. Maiko’s brand menu varies by location, so confirm the local selection and hours before going. Prices are published menu prices before tax and extras; menu listings do not guarantee stock.

Usucha or Matcha Latte

Best Boy Electric

1746 Post St · Japantown · San Francisco

For traditional hand-whisked tea or an unsweetened latte.

At 1746 Post St, inside NEW PEOPLE, the menu lists single-origin usucha with a small snack and a steamed-milk matcha latte made unsweetened by default. There is also a hand-whisked matcha americano with hot or iced water.

Milk details
The latte uses steamed milk; specific plant-milk choices are not shown on the current public menu. The americano is described as matcha and water. Check the accompanying snack’s ingredients if ordering usucha.
Soy status
Specific plant-milk choices are not named on the current public ordering menu; ask about soy.
Price
Usucha $6; Matcha Latte $6.50; Matcha Americano $6
Availability
NEW PEOPLE posts Mon–Fri 8am–3:30pm and Sat–Sun 9am–5pm. The ordering menu marked drinks unavailable when checked; confirm in-person availability.
Hours
Mon–Fri 8am–3:30pm; Sat–Sun 9am–5pm

Matcha Latte or Iced Banana Matcha Latte

YakiniQ Cafe

1640 Post St, 1st floor · Japantown · San Francisco

For a classic latte with milk options or a fruit-matcha menu.

Find YakiniQ at 1640 Post St, first floor. Its menu lists a classic matcha latte alongside iced banana, strawberry, red bean and lavender versions. Matcha Tea and Matcha Fizz are also listed.

Milk details
The classic latte’s customization panel lists oat, almond and soy at +$0.75, labelled “Extra” milk. Ask for a replacement of dairy rather than an addition. Do not assume the banana or cream drinks use the same recipe or substitutions.
Soy status
Soy milk is available for the Matcha Latte for $0.75 extra.
Price
Matcha Latte from $7; Iced Banana Matcha Latte $8.50
Availability
Tue–Sat 11am–5pm; closed Sunday and Monday.
Hours
Tue–Sat 11am–5pm; Sun–Mon closed

A Japan Center stop for matcha drinks and desserts.

Maiko’s official store page places its Japantown shop at 1581 Webster St, Suite 175. The brand menu lists matcha lattes, matcha soft serve, parfaits and tea floats. Confirm which items this counter is serving before deciding on a specific dessert.

Before you go

Where can I order matcha without milk in Japantown?

Best Boy Electric’s menu describes a Matcha Americano made with hot or iced water, and lists usucha separately from its milk lattes. YakiniQ also lists Matcha Tea; check sweeteners and the complete recipe when ordering.

Which Japantown matcha café lists soy?

YakiniQ’s classic Matcha Latte ordering panel lists soy at +$0.75. It is labelled “Extra Soy Milk,” so confirm that dairy will be replaced rather than added to. We could not freshly confirm soy choices on Best Boy’s or Maiko’s current public menu.

Can I visit these cafés on Sunday?

YakiniQ’s ordering page says it is closed Sunday. NEW PEOPLE lists Best Boy Electric’s Sunday hours as 9am–5pm. Maiko asks visitors to call its Japantown store for current hours.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
