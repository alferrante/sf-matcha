Matcha in San Francisco’s Mission District: Five Valencia & 20th Street Stops

Five documented Mission matcha menus, from traditional and floral lattes to fruit and cream-top builds.

Updated October 9, 2026

The Mission has a compact run of matcha menus along Valencia Street and nearby 20th and 21st Streets. Stonemill focuses on Japanese tea and café food; Binge and Ceré stretch into fruit, ube and cream-top drinks; Tadaima pairs matcha with sandos; and Marigold lists a floral lavender latte. This is an unranked route organized by menu style and location.

We checked official café sites, menus, location pages and current merchant ordering pages on October 9, 2026. Published prices can differ from in-store prices or include delivery markup. Menu listings do not guarantee stock, and a plant-milk choice does not establish that a foam or topping is dairy-free.

Before you go

Is this a ranking of the Mission’s matcha cafés?

No. The stops are grouped as an unranked route using current menu evidence, location and ordering details rather than firsthand taste scores.

Which Mission stop lists soy milk?

Ceré lists soy for matcha drinks. The other current menus in this guide list oat and/or almond, or do not name soy; confirm the full drink and topping recipe.

Can plant milk still come with dairy foam?

Yes. A base-milk substitution does not change every topping. Ask separately about cream, cheese foam and cold foam before ordering.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
