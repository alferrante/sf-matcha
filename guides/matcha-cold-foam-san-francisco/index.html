Matcha Cold Foam, Clouds & Cream Tops in SF

Four places for matcha clouds and cream tops in San Francisco, with prices, seasonal availability and dairy notes.

Updated October 7, 2026

The foam is part of the order, not just a garnish. San Francisco cafés offer matcha with flavored cream, matcha-flavored clouds, and fruit-topped lattes. Start with these four documented menus, which span an extra layer of matcha, salted cheese cream, a Korean café cream top and an autumn London Fog twist. If you choose a plant milk underneath, check the topping separately.

Official menus checked October 7, 2026. “Cold foam,” “cloud” and “cream top” are café menu terms, not a claim that every drink uses the same recipe. This guide is unranked and does not award a Best Cold Foam badge.

Before you go

Does oat milk make a cloud matcha dairy-free?

Only if the topping and other ingredients are dairy-free too. Ask about the foam independently from the milk base.

Which menus have published prices?

Q, Tadaima and SŌHN publish the prices shown here. Kumo’s official menu describes the drink without a price.

Where is the Best Cold Foam winner?

This is a menu guide. A tasting-based award will be labeled separately with its judging method.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
