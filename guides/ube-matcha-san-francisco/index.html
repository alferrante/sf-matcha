Ube Matcha in San Francisco: Four Menus That List It

Four documented ube-matcha drinks compared by format: cold foam, latte, snow top and affogato.

Updated October 9, 2026

Ube matcha is not one standard drink. In San Francisco, current menus use purple yam in cold foam, layered lattes, snow tops and an affogato. These four documented options are organized by format, not ranked by taste.

We checked official menus, official social posts and current merchant pages on October 9, 2026. Delivery prices may include markup. Where a menu does not disclose soy or dairy details, the guide says so rather than inferring ingredients.

Before you go

Is ube matcha always dairy-free?

No. Binge’s current menu explicitly says its coconut-ube cold foam contains dairy. The other menus do not publish complete ingredient details, so ask about every layer.

Which ube matcha listing names soy milk?

Maruwu lists soy among latte milk choices, but the snow-top recipe still needs a separate ingredient check. The other three item listings do not confirm soy.

Are these the best ube matchas in San Francisco?

This is a source-backed list of documented menu options, not a firsthand tasting ranking or award.

Menus, prices, and hours can change. Check the linked café sources before making a special trip; ask the café about allergies and shared preparation.
